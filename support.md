# PulseHalo Support

Last updated: April 30, 2026

## 1. Product scope

PulseHalo is an ambient nighttime utility app focused on:

- night mode selection
- timed playback
- scene-based usage guidance
- subscription access
- local usage records

The app is intended to provide an organized and polished nighttime utility experience on iPhone.

## 2. Common questions

### Does the app require an account?

No. The current version does not require account creation or sign in to access the core experience.

### Does the app make medical or guaranteed claims?

No. PulseHalo is positioned as an ambient nighttime utility. It does not claim medical efficacy, certified scientific measurement, guaranteed real-world outcomes, or replacement of physical protection products.

### How are subscriptions handled?

Subscription products and in-app purchases are managed by Apple through the App Store. You can restore purchases inside the app and manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

### What if prices do not appear immediately on first launch?

On first launch, the app may briefly sync App Store product information before showing live pricing. Purchase actions remain visible, and the app provides on-screen feedback while product data is loading.

### What does the app store locally?

The current version may store preferences such as selected mode, timer duration, subscription status display, interface preferences, and local usage-style records on device.

Help for night modes, timed sessions, local records, subscription restore, and product-scope questions.

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Product Scope

TAME PulseHalo is an ambient nighttime utility focused on night mode selection, timed playback, scene-based usage guidance, subscription access, and local usage records.

Common Questions

Does the app require an account?

No. The current version does not require account creation or sign in to access the core experience.

Does the app make medical or outcome claims?

No. TAME PulseHalo is positioned as an ambient nighttime utility. It does not claim medical efficacy, certified scientific measurement, specific real-world outcomes, or replacement of physical protection products.

How are subscriptions handled?

Subscription products and in-app purchases are managed by Apple through the App Store. You can restore purchases inside the app and manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

What does the app store locally?

The current version may store preferences such as selected mode, timer duration, subscription status display, interface preferences, and local usage-style records on device.

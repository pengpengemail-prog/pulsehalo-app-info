Last updated: July 30, 2026

Product Scope

TAME PulseHalo is an audio mode and timer utility focused on mode selection, timed playback, current-session planning suggestions, and Apple-managed Pro subscription access.

Common Questions

Does the app require an account?

No. The current version does not require account creation or sign in to access the core experience.

Does the app make medical or outcome claims?

No. TAME PulseHalo does not claim medical efficacy, pest-control efficacy, certified scientific measurement, specific real-world outcomes, or replacement of physical protection products.

How are purchases handled?

Monthly and yearly Pro subscriptions are managed by Apple through the App Store. You can restore eligible purchases inside the app and manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

What does the app store locally?

The current version may store preferences such as selected mode, timer duration, purchase status display, and interface preferences on device. The Plan screen derives suggestions from the current time, selected mode, and timer; it is not a history or sensor record.
